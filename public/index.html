About

Honestly? The selling part isn’t really why I’m here. Though I suppose it validates the work somehow. And it helps keep the lights on.

I shoot because I love it. Simple as that.

Most of these images were made in Switzerland, where I lived for five years, and along the Carolina coast, where I live now.

There are moments when I can see the finished image before I’ve even pressed the shutter — the light, the frame, the feeling. And then there are the happy accidents, the shots that break every rule and somehow work better because of it. Imperfection has a way of creating its own composition.

So I hope you enjoy a few. And if you’re moved to purchase something, I trust my friend has designed the website to make it easy to happen.

And I thank you. And Otis thanks you.

Every print is made to order on archival paper, gallery-wrapped canvas, or aluminum, in up to three different sizes. Choose a photograph, choose how you want it to hang, and it ships ready for the wall.
