Privacy

Last updated November 19, 2026

cotton.gallery is the print shop of photographer Bruce Cotton, operated by Blended Cotton, LLC in Wilmington, North Carolina. This page explains what information we collect when you visit or buy a print, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, email address, phone number, and shipping address. You enter these on Stripe’s secure checkout page.
  • Payment details: your card or wallet details go directly to Stripe, our payment processor. They never reach our website, and we never see or store your full card number.
  • When you browse: our web host, Netlify, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) for security and reliability.

How we use it

Only to fulfill your order: to take payment, print and ship your artwork, calculate sales tax, send your receipt, and contact you about your order if needed. We don’t send marketing email unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe processes payments and calculates sales tax.
  • White House Custom Colour (WHCC), our professional print lab, receives your name and shipping address so it can print and ship your order directly to you.
  • Netlify hosts this website.

We don’t sell, rent, or trade your information, and we don’t use advertising or tracking services.

Cookies and your browser

This site doesn’t use tracking cookies. Your shopping cart is saved only in your own browser so it’s still there if you come back. Stripe’s checkout page uses its own cookies to process payments securely and prevent fraud.

How long we keep it

We keep order records as long as needed for the order, for customer service, and for tax and accounting requirements.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, except where we must keep records by law. Email us and we’ll take care of it.
