Returns

Last updated November 19, 2026

Every print is made to order for you by our professional lab, so we can’t accept returns or exchanges for a change of mind. We want you to love what arrives, though, and we’ll make it right if anything goes wrong.

Damaged or defective prints

If your print arrives damaged, or has a defect in printing or construction, email a photo of the problem and of the packaging within 14 days of delivery. We’ll send a replacement at no cost to you. There’s no need to ship the damaged piece back unless we ask.

Cancellations

You can cancel an order within 24 hours of purchase, before it goes to the lab, for a full refund. After production begins, orders can’t be cancelled.

Shipping

Shipping within the United States is free. Prints are made to order and usually arrive within 7 to 14 business days. We currently ship to U.S. addresses only.

Color

Screens vary, so colors in print may look slightly different from what you see on your display. That’s not considered a defect.
